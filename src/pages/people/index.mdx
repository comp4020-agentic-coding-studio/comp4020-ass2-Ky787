Three people, one of whom is only here for Block IV. Ask the convenor about the
course, the tutor about anything you can put on a screen, and the guest about
whether your conclusion survives contact with somebody whose job is doubting
it.

<PeopleGrid />

## Who to ask about what

- **A specimen, a listing, a tool that will not cooperate** — the tutor, in the
  studio. Bring the file open.
- **Marking, extensions, the shape of the course** — the convenor, by email.
- **Whether your capstone scope is realistic** — the convenor, in week 10,
  before you have built anything on the assumption that it is.
- **Lab image access, disassembler licences** — the tutor.

Consultation is the half hour after each studio, in the room. There is no
separate booking system; if that half hour does not work for you, email the
convenor and something will be arranged.
