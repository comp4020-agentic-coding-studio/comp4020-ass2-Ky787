This course puts its teaching hours in the studio. Twelve two-hour studios are
where the evidence actually gets read, and every one of them has a page on this
site carrying the material, not a link to a slide dump.

What the lectures are for is the thing a studio is bad at: saying what the next
three weeks are *for* before you are elbow-deep in a disassembly listing, and
saying afterwards what changed. So there are four, one per block, delivered in
weeks 1, 4, 7 and 10.

Each carries a deck, and each deck is the deck that was delivered — the numbers
on the slides are the numbers in the retained result files, and the slides say
where each came from. If a claim on a slide is not on a week page with its
evidence beside it, treat the slide as wrong.

## Recordings and attendance

Lectures are recorded and posted with the deck. Attendance is not marked;
nothing in the assessment depends on being in the room. The studios are a
different matter, because you cannot read someone else's notes on a
disassembly listing and get anything out of it.
