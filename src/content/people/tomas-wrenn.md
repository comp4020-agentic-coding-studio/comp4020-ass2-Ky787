Keeps the lab: the Windows x64 image where the twenty-one retained specimens
can actually be executed and stepped, the disassembler licences, and the
shared notes on which of them are safe to run and which exist only to be read.

Spends most of the studio hours on the same question — *what would you have to
check before you believed that?* — applied to whatever a tool has just put on
your screen. The most common finding in weeks 3 to 5 is that an expression was
already simple before the simplifier ran, and the second most common is that a
count was taken with two different conventions and then subtracted.

Marks the practical half of A1 and A2, and will tell you in week 2 that an
annotated screenshot with no file hash beside it is worth nothing.
