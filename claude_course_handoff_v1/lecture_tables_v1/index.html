TABLE 06

What does each deobfuscator actually produce?

Expression recovery, a branch proof, LLVM IR and a rewritten PE are different outputs.

WorkflowInput / ideaDelivered resultBoundary to remember
msynth AST/oracleExtracted expression; rewrite / matchSimpler expressionNo rewritten PE in this study.
SiMBAAlgebraic MBA simplificationSimpler expressionPartial reduction is not full recovery.
SynthesizerSearch for a small expressionCandidate expressionAccept only after equivalence proof.
ollvm-unflattenerDiscover states; recover successorsRewritten PE or failureA produced PE can have wrong semantics.
Triton DSE/SMTSymbolic branch conditionsProof / witnessAnalysis itself does not patch the PE.
Proof-driven rewriterConsume proofs and exact bytesPatched PEPredicates and dead bytes can remain.
MergenLift x64 instructionsLLVM IRValid IR can silently miscompute.
Ordinary LLVM O2Optimize retained or lifted IROptimized IRCannot repair an unsound lift.

Ask two questions: what representation was recovered, and how was its correctness checked?

PE = Windows Portable Executable. The Mergen test harness is not a rewritten original PE.
