# Research summary

The research compared recognizable C semantics with transformed Windows x64 binaries and then tested what different deobfuscation methods actually recover. This pack selects six core stories plus two Tigress examples; exact originals, hashes, source, IR, CFGs and retained validation remain traceable. It is evidence for later course design, not a new corpus or a course schedule.

1. **The transformation layer matters.** Tigress changes C before compilation; OLLVM/Hikari/Polaris change LLVM IR; Polaris also has a separate X86 MIR/backend path; BinProtect changes a linked PE. The same mathematical idea can therefore meet different later optimizations. See [pipelines](PIPELINES.md) and [family comparison](tables/obfuscators.md).

2. **Optimization is part of the experiment.** Classic SUB can look elaborate in IR while the backend recovers native operations. Hikari/Polaris BCF disappears by O1 in the measured target; OLLVM leaves a small residue. Flattening largely survives, with altered dispatch forms. Tigress arithmetic falls from 426 O0 to 226 /Ox target instructions, still above its matched 106-instruction /Ox clean baseline. See [optimization table](tables/optimization_survival.md).

3. **Arithmetic simplification is not executable recovery.** msynth/SiMBA/synthesis can recover selected local expressions, but do not emit rewritten PEs here. Whole-function extraction and proof budgets limit transfer. Polaris MBA is an especially clear boundary: 115 to 678 native instructions, successful Mergen lifting, yet substantial arithmetic remains after LLVM optimization. See stories [1](manual_analysis/01_ollvm_sub/README.md), [2](manual_analysis/02_polaris_mba/README.md) and [7](manual_analysis/07_tigress_arithmetic/README.md).

4. **Semantic methods can transfer across implementations.** The generic Triton workflow proved 22 OLLVM and 16 Polaris opaque gates. A separate proof consumer made layout-preserving branch patches; retained native differential checks found zero mismatches across 9189 triples. Predicate arithmetic and dead bytes remain, so this is working partial deobfuscation, not complete cleanup. See stories [3](manual_analysis/03_ollvm_bcf/README.md) and [4](manual_analysis/04_polaris_bcf/README.md).

5. **Analysis assumptions determine failure modes.** Stock unflattening recovers OLLVM/Hikari O0 successor structure, but Tigress fails during jump-table CFG discovery before symbolic analysis. Tigress opaque-predicate proofs require captured initialized state. These are different limits, not a single tool success/failure score. See [capability matrix](tables/end_to_end_results.md).

6. **Smaller or well-formed output can still be wrong.** Polaris FLA shrinks from 56 to 10 blocks while collapsing a legitimate switch and returning the wrong result. Mergen emits valid, compilable LLVM IR for OLLVM SUB but miscomputes 3917/4118 tested vectors per stage. The marked incorrect PE and retained lift failure are deliberate negative controls; correctness must accompany visual simplification. See story [6](manual_analysis/06_polaris_fla_incorrect/README.md) and [mergen_validation](reference/research_docs/mergen_validation.md).

7. **Exact artifacts and scoped claims are essential.** Seeds do not guarantee reproducibility for Polaris MBA/MIR or whole Tigress generated files. All eight BinProtect protected outputs were invalid, so they support static observations only. Hashes, compatible clean baselines and symbol warnings prevent these cases from being promoted into unsupported success claims. See [manifest](MANIFEST.json) and [walkthrough](IDA_WALKTHROUGH.md).
