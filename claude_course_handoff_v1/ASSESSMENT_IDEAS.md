# Assessment ideas — provisional

No assessment weighting is assigned. No institutional rubric or mandatory weighting was identified in the supplied brief/current documentation. Confirm contact hours, prerequisites, tool access and assessment rules with the course owner before finalizing these ideas. They assess analysis, not implementation of an entire deobfuscator.

| Idea | Skills assessed | Prerequisite weeks | Likely deliverable | Why suitable / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Trace one operation across C, IR and assembly | Dataflow, width/overflow awareness, distinction between compiler and obfuscator effects | 1–3 | Annotated three-representation excerpt and a short semantic explanation | Small bounded task using [OLLVM SUB IR](assets/ir/s01_ollvm_sub/target_function.ll) and [native disassembly](assets/evidence/s01_ollvm_sub/demo_substitution_disassembly.txt); readable without proprietary tools. |
| B. Judge an opaque-branch argument | State assumptions, SAT/UNSAT, path-relative implication, genuine-branch controls | 6–7 | Proof-obligation worksheet with supported/unsupported conclusions | Use [frozen first-gate proof](assets/evidence/s03_ollvm_bcf/first_gate_proof/result.json) and Tigress initialized/generic results; no solver installation required. |
| C. Recover a flattened function's structure | Dispatcher/state identification, semantic regions, successor reasoning | 9 | Annotated CFG and a small state/successor table, not a full rewriter | [OLLVM comparison](assets/cfg/s04_ollvm_fla/course_comparison.svg) provides independent source/IR ground truth. Distinguish case labels from actual runtime state updates. |
| D. Audit two deobfuscation claims | Correctness evidence, failure localization, metadata awareness | 7–11 | Evidence-backed verdict comparing successful OLLVM FLA and wrong Polaris FLA or passing/failing Mergen lifts | Forces separation of output validity, visual simplicity and tested semantics; [claims guide](CLAIMS_AND_CAVEATS.md) supplies bounded statements. |
| E. Small capstone: unfamiliar retained sample | Multi-representation synthesis, justified tool choice, limitations and reproducibility | 1–11 | Short analysis notebook/report with provenance hashes, observations, a proposed semantic test and a bounded conclusion | Instructor can withhold a copied retained case's label/solution in a separate teaching derivative; do not alter this handoff or require new corpus generation. Ground truth and negative controls already exist. |
| F. Critical reflection: why smaller is not enough | Argument quality, counterexamples, generalization limits | 5, 8, 10–12 | Concise position paper comparing two failures and one success, with exact evidence links | Polaris wrong FLA, Mergen silent mislift and working branch patches prevent a tool-success checklist from replacing reasoning. |

Suggested qualitative criteria: accurate semantics; traceable evidence; correct comparator and metric scope; explicit uncertainty; clear explanation of what was not established. Reward identifying UNKNOWN and missing provenance, not only reporting success. Do not penalize students for lacking optional commercial tooling; provide static/text routes.
